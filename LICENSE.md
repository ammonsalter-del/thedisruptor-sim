## 3. Statement of Fictional Elements

Everything in the simulation's world is **fictional** and should be treated as such in any use or derivative work.

- **Vélox Dynamics** — the Belgian fitness-equipment company at the centre of the game — does not exist. It is invented for pedagogical purposes.
- **The Vélox founders, employees, board members, customers, and competitors** — including all named individuals, organisations, and institutions appearing inside the game — are fictional. Any resemblance to real persons, living or dead, or to real organisations, is coincidental and unintended.
- **The Vélox products** — including CycleSense R1, VéloxConnect, VéloxHome, Vélox Allez, Vélox+, Vélox Band, Vélox Band Pro, Vélox Essentials Treadmill, Vélox Home Gym, and any other product or service referenced — are fictional. They are not available for purchase and do not correspond to real products.
- **Events, scenarios, regulatory references, market statistics, and competitor moves** inside the game are illustrative constructions, not reports of real events.
- **The financial figures, staff numbers, product histories, and company performance** shown in the game are invented for pedagogical realism and do not reflect any real firm.

**Derivative works, teaching materials, videos, articles, podcasts, infographics, and any other content generated from this simulation must preserve the fictional nature of the Vélox world.** Such content must not represent Vélox Dynamics, its products, its people, or its events as real. Any comparison to real-world firms should be explicitly framed as analogy or reference, never as equivalence.

The innovation-management concepts embedded in the game and the scholars credited for them are real and well-established. Treating those concepts as real is correct and expected. Treating the Vélox world as real is not.
